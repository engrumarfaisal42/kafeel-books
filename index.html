🔗 Connect Google Drive — ربط جوجل درايف

Your transaction data will auto-save to a file in your Google Drive after every entry. Works like a live backup.

1
Click Sign in with Google below — a Google login window will open. Sign in with your Gmail account.
2
Allow the app to access Google Drive files — this only creates/updates one file named Kafeel_Books_1446.json in your Drive.
3
After connecting, every transaction you add auto-saves instantly to Google Drive. You can open this app on any device and restore your data from Drive.
🔒 Your data stays in YOUR Google Drive. The app never sends data to any other server.
